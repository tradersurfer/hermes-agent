# BA Style Rules (condensed from "Bitcoin Archive Style Guide 2.pdf")

## Voice & Tone
- Plain, punchy, confident. Short sentences. No hedging.
- Author byline is always "By Archie".
- NO X hashtags inside article body (hashtags belong on X, not the site).
- Section labels use the orange (#f7931a) brand color.

## Article structure (NEWS — quick read, 2–3 min)
1. Orange eyebrow = CATEGORY (BITCOIN / CHARTS / MARKETS / WORLD / TREASURIES /
   MINING & ENERGY / ADOPTION & PAYMENTS / CULTURE).
2. H1 headline (specific, news-driven).
3. Byline: "Published <date> · By Archie".
4. KEY TAKEAWAY box (orange left border) — 2 short paragraphs, the gist up top.
5. Body in short sections (### subheads OK). 2–3 min read.
6. Pulled X posts as styled cards (handle + text + "via X" + link).
7. Sources & References list.

## Article structure (TRENDING — deeper)
- Same shell but longer, more analysis, may include a comparison TABLE.
- Uses the BA orange/black palette; can embed charts/images from
  C:\Users\jorda\Downloads\Bitcoin Archive.

## Verification rule (user's words)
- "verify, don't fabricate" — pull real figures; if a number can't be verified,
  phrase it as a general established fact rather than inventing it.
- BUT never ship an empty shell with [VERIFY] placeholders — the user rejected that.
  Real written body + real pulled X posts are mandatory.
